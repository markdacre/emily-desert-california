How we work · three steps

No templates. No rush.
Just a plan that fits your body.

01
Step 01

We talk

A free 20-minute call. You tell me what's going on — the injury, the pain, the thing you miss doing. I listen, ask a lot of questions, and tell you honestly whether I'm the right person to help.

02
Step 02

We build your plan

I write a program specifically for your body, your history, and your goal. Not a template. If you've been told to 'just be careful' or 'stop doing the thing you love', we start there — and we work back toward it.

03
Step 03

We get to work

We train together, in the studio or online. I adjust the plan week by week based on what your body is telling us. My job is to make you more capable than you were — and to get out of your way once you are.
